Cash vs accrual is a timing decision: cash shows when money moved, accrual shows when the business earned it or owed it.

This guide compares the two methods for small businesses. I have kept books on both methods for years.

Process Overview

Cash basis records revenue when the customer pays. Accrual records it when the work is done.

Hard Rule: Pick one method for the books and use it every month. Switching back and forth makes the trend meaningless.

Choosing a Method

Look at inventory, customer payment terms and who reads your reports. Additionally, talk to your tax adviser before you change the method on a return.

FAQ

Can a small business use cash basis?

Often, yes. Lenders and investors usually want accrual statements, though.

Closing Thoughts

Choose the method your readers need, not the one that is easiest this month. Then keep it.
