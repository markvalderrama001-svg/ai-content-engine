Reconciling a business credit card means matching every charge to a receipt and a ledger entry each month. It can be a quick task for a small team — but it may need more structure than you think.

In my experience reconciling cards for small businesses, the problems start in the first month. You can export statements straight into RivalLedger Software most of the time.

Process Overview

A typical card has 40 to 120 charges a month. Each one needs a receipt, a category and a match to the statement.

Setup

Give every cardholder a receipt deadline of three business days. Post the statement to a clearing account, not straight to expense. A clean chart of accounts keeps the categories consistent.

Our chart of accounts guide covers the setup steps.

Common Errors

Duplicate charges sometimes slip through. Match on amount and date, not just the vendor name.

FAQ

How often should a business credit card be reconciled?

Every month, within five business days of the statement date.

Does every card charge need a receipt?

Yes. Keep a receipt for every charge, with the business purpose written on it.

Closing Thoughts

Set the deadline, match every line and tie the balance to the statement. The card stops being a monthly surprise.
