A month-end close is a relay race: every handoff has a deadline, and one late leg stalls the whole team.

This checklist is for small finance teams that close the books every month. I run the close for a team of five on a five-day calendar.

Process Overview

A clean close takes five business days. It's worth noting that the first close on a new checklist usually takes eight.

Hard Rule: Nothing is closed until it ties to a source document. A balance without support is a guess.

Close Calendar

Day 1 is cutoff and bank reconciliations, day 3 is accruals and review, and day 5 is sign-off. Moreover, every task has one owner and a different reviewer.

FAQ

How long should a month-end close take?

Five business days is a fair target for a team of five.

Who signs off the close?

The controller signs off. Every reconciliation has a preparer and a separate reviewer.

Closing Thoughts

Own the calendar, not the heroics. A boring close is the whole point.
